Seven questions · about two minutes

Can you say what every one of your AI agents is allowed to do?

Most teams can answer that for the agents they built on purpose. The check is about the rest: where the rules actually live, and what happens the day somebody needs them changed.

Question 1 of 7

Nothing to govern yet

A person approves every step, so the person is the control

This check measures how you govern agents that act on their own. While someone signs off each action, that someone is your policy engine, and it works. It stops working the first time an agent is trusted to act unattended, because the rules then have to live somewhere other than a person's judgement.

Worth knowing before that day

The cheapest moment to decide where your agent rules live is before any agent needs them. Retrofitting control onto agents already running in production is the expensive version, and it is the version most teams end up doing.
