No agents in production means nothing to govern yet

This check measures how you govern agents that already act on your systems and your data. While nothing is running, there is no posture to score, and any number it produced would be about a future you have not built yet.

Worth knowing before that changes

The cheapest moment to decide where agent policy lives is before the first agent needs it. Retrofitting control onto agents already running in production is the expensive version, and it is the version most teams end up doing.
